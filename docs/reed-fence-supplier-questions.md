# Reed fence series — supplier questions

We're building a separate web page for each reed-fence type. For Google/AI search we need
**factual, specific** answers — what each version is, what makes it different, and who it's for.
Please answer as plainly as possible (numbers where you can). Estimates are fine — just mark them.

The series (6 versions):

1. **Extra-thick peeled reed (8–10 mm)**
2. **Thick peeled reed (5–8 mm)**
3. **High-density fine peeled reed (3–6 mm)**
4. **Unpeeled thick reed (natural skin on)**
5. **Half-round / split reed**
6. **Japanese-style reed bound with bamboo**

## Part B — "Why choose this one" (1–2 sentences per version)

For **each** of the 6, please answer:

1. **In one line, what is this version and what makes it different from the others?**
2. **Why would a customer pick this one specifically?** (the single biggest reason)
3. **What is the main trade-off vs the neighbouring versions?** (e.g. cheaper but thinner, or denser but heavier)
4. **Who is it NOT right for?**

Version-specific things we'd like clarified:

- **Extra-thick peeled (8–10 mm)** — Is this the most private/"most closed" option? Heaviest? Longest-lasting?
- **Thick peeled (5–8 mm)** — Is this the everyday best-seller? What makes it the "default" choice?
- **High-density fine (3–6 mm)** — Why choose fine reed? Is it about a smoother, more refined look rather than privacy? Is it more or less closed than the thick peeled?
- **Unpeeled thick reed** — What does keeping the skin on change in practice (look, lifespan, mould risk, smell, price)? Is it cheaper or dearer than peeled?
- **Half-round / split reed** — Why split the reed? Flatter face, less depth, lower shipping weight, lower price? Is it as private as round reed?
- **Japanese-style with bamboo** — What bamboo is used for the binding, and what's the binding pattern? Is this mainly decorative, or also full privacy? Premium-priced?

## Part C — Series-level questions (answer once for the whole range)

1. **Which version is your best-seller**, and which is the cheapest / which is the most premium?
2. **Which is the most "closed" — no gaps, maximum privacy** — and which lets the most light/air through?
3. **Peeled vs unpeeled in real terms** — difference in lifespan, mould resistance, smell, and how the colour changes over the years?
4. **Which versions are safe near a swimming pool / on the coast** (salt, chlorine, constant damp)?
5. **How is each one fixed** to a fence/railing (wire ties, battens)? Any difference between versions?
6. **Any version that is heavier/denser** and therefore needs a stronger supporting frame?

### Why we ask these (for our side)

Each fact above becomes either a spec line or an **FAQ answer** on that product's page. The questions
that buyers actually type — *"Can you see through reed fencing?"*, *"How long does reed fencing last?"*,
*"What's the difference between peeled and unpeeled reed?"*, *"Which reed fence gives the most privacy?"*,
*"Is reed fencing wind-resistant?"* — are answered directly on the page (and tagged as FAQ data so Google
and AI assistants can quote them). That's why the **opacity / privacy**, **lifespan**, and **why-choose**
answers matter most.
