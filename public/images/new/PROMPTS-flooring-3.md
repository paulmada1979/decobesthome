# Flooring-in-rooms prompts — 50 modern interiors

**How to use:** attach your **bamboo flooring sample** image, then copy a whole block.
Each block tells the model to lay *that exact sample* as the room's floor. Save results like
`floor-banner-04.png`, `floor-support-21.png`.

Shared rule baked into every block: *match the sample's plank colour, grain, width, sheen and
direction faithfully; it is the real installed floor, in sharp focus, covering the foreground.*
